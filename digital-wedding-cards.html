Questions

Good to know

What is a wedding card website?

Instead of a photo or PDF, your invitation is a link. Guests tap to open a cinematic video, then scroll through events, photos and RSVP. It works on any phone, with no app to install.

How much does a digital wedding card website cost?

Designs start at ₹2,500 (Marigold). Rajasthan Wedding is ₹3,000 and the premium Udaipur Palace design is ₹10,000. All are one-time prices.

How do I order?

Tap Order on WhatsApp on any design, pay by UPI, and send us your names, date, venue and photos. We build your website and send you the link.

How long does it take?

Your invitation is ready within 48 hours after payment and details.

Can I ask for changes?

2 rounds of changes are included.
