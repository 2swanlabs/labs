Questions about Rajasthan Wedding

What is the Rajasthan Wedding wedding card website?

It is a digital wedding invitation built as a website. Guests tap to open a cinematic video invitation, then scroll through your events, the couple’s photos and an RSVP. Rajasthan Wedding is the design: royal & colourful.

How much does it cost?

The Rajasthan Wedding wedding card website costs ₹3,000, a one-time price with no hidden charges.
